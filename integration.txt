ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
============================================================

EnginePipeline orchestrates engines in order:
  1. ReferenceEngine   — computes ReferencePrice via IReferenceModel
  2. DistanceEngine    — computes DirectionalExtension & AbsoluteExtension
  3. ScaleEngine       — computes CharacteristicScale via IScaleModel
  4. NormalizationEngine — normalizes measurements via INormalizationModel

Values flow through shared EngineContext.Values (EngineValues).
